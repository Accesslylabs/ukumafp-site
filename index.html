uKumaFP

Personal finance planner · 個人理財規劃

English·繁體中文·Privacy Policy

A personal finance planner for your own computer, with an iPhone app for looking at the summary.

What it does

  • On Windows: keeps your accounts, holdings, loans, spending and goals, and works out your net worth and future cash flow.
  • On iPhone: shows the summary from your computer, with current prices. It only displays; it cannot change anything.

It does not place trades or move money.

Why it asks for Google sign-in

So that your computer and your iPhone can share one summary file, kept in a hidden space in your own Google Drive. The app cannot see your other files, and there is no server of ours in between. See the Privacy Policy.

Availability

uKumaFP is a personal tool. It is not for sale and is not on the App Store.

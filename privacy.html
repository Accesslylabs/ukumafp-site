English·繁體中文·Home

Last updated: 5 November 2026

uKumaFP has no server, and we never receive your data.
Google sign-in is used only so that your own computer and your own iPhone can share one summary file, kept in a hidden space in your own Google Drive.

What the app accesses in your Google account

  • App data in your Google Drive (drive.appdata): the computer app creates and updates one file, ukumafp-summary.json, in the hidden folder Google gives each app. The iPhone app reads that file. The app cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Your email address (openid email): shown in the app so you can see which account is connected. It stays on your device.

How that data is used

Only to show you your own summary on your iPhone. It is not sent to us, because we do not operate a server. It is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to develop, improve or train AI models.

uKumaFP's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What is in the summary

The names and balances of the accounts and loans you entered, your holdings, your own investment rules and goals, and your to-do items. It has no account numbers, no individual trades and no ledger entries.

Where it is stored

  • In the hidden app folder in your own Google Drive.
  • On your iPhone, in the iOS Keychain, on that device only. It does not sync to iCloud.
  • On your computer, in the app's data folder. The Google sign-in token is encrypted where Windows allows it.

Other connections

To show current prices, the app sends stock tickers and fund identifiers to public price sources (Taiwan Stock Exchange, Yahoo Finance and cnyes). It never sends how many shares you hold, or any amount.

The computer app has an optional assistant, KumaFP. It runs on the AI subscription you sign in to yourself (Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT) and sends that service computed figures when you use it. Nothing read from Google Drive is sent to it.

The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

How to remove your data

  • Sign out in the app: this removes the sign-in and the copy on that device.
  • Delete the file in Google Drive: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings, Manage apps, find uKumaFP, and choose Delete hidden app data.
  • Revoke access: at your Google account's third-party access page.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.

App 會用到你 Google 帳號的哪些東西

  • 雲端硬碟裡這個 App 專用的空間（drive.appdata）：電腦版在 Google 給每個 App 的隱藏資料夾裡，建立和更新一個檔案 ukumafp-summary.json，iPhone 版讀這個檔案。App 看不到你雲端硬碟裡的其他檔案。
  • 你的電子郵件地址（openid email）：顯示在 App 裡，讓你知道連的是哪個帳號。只留在你的裝置上。

這些資料拿來做什麼

只用來在你的 iPhone 上顯示你自己的摘要。不會傳給我們，因為我們沒有伺服器。不販售、不分享給任何人、不用於廣告，也不用來開發、改進或訓練 AI 模型。

uKumaFP 對從 Google API 取得的資訊的使用與傳輸，遵守 Google API 服務使用者資料政策，包含其中的「限制使用」規定。

摘要裡有什麼

你輸入的帳戶和貸款的名稱與餘額、持股、你自己的投資規則和目標、該做的事。沒有帳號、沒有交易明細，也沒有記帳明細。

存在哪裡

  • 你自己的 Google 雲端硬碟裡的隱藏空間。
  • 你的 iPhone 的鑰匙圈，只留在這支手機，不跟 iCloud 同步。
  • 你的電腦裡 App 的資料夾。Google 登入憑證在 Windows 允許時會加密。

其他連線

為了顯示最新價格，App 會把股票代號和基金代碼傳給公開的報價來源（臺灣證券交易所、Yahoo Finance、鉅亨網）。不會傳持股數量或任何金額。

電腦版有一個可以不用的助手 KumaFP，用的是你自己登入的 AI 訂閱（Claude、Gemini 或 ChatGPT），使用時會把算好的數字傳給那個服務。從 Google 雲端硬碟讀到的東西不會傳給它。

App 沒有任何分析工具、廣告或追蹤。

怎麼刪除資料

  • 在 App 裡登出：清掉那台裝置上的登入和資料。
  • 刪除雲端硬碟裡的檔案：Google 雲端硬碟網頁的設定、管理應用程式，找到 uKumaFP，選刪除隱藏的應用程式資料。
  • 取消授權：到 Google 帳戶的第三方存取權頁面。
